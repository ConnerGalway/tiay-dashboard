Data matters. But the numbers alone often don't tell the full story. Access to industry data in the Yukon has improved tremendously, with the Yukon government's Tourism Indicators Dashboard providing update information on a regular basis.

To complement that, TIA Yukon's new twice-yearly Industry Insights Report draws on this dashboard and other available data to put trends into context, explore what they mean for your business and help shape conversations about Yukon tourism's future.

We encourage you to explore the available data, contribute to industry surveys and sectoral reporting, and share your insights directly with us. Your participation helps build a stronger understanding of our industry and the realities of running a tourism business in the Yukon.

Here's to seeing the bigger picture, together. Enjoy our first edition!

Caroline Anderson, Executive Director, TIA Yukon

Hotels raised rates rather than filling rooms

78%occupancy, May to Sept
$260average room rate
+14%rate vs summer 2025

Hotel occupancy slipped two points from last summer while the average room rate climbed 14%. This season's hotel growth came from rate, not from more rooms sold.

Short-term rentals moved differently. Listings, occupancy and rates all rose, and rental revenue grew by about a quarter. The two accommodation markets are growing in different ways.

Why it matters: When hotels grow through pricing and rentals grow through volume, it changes the competitive picture. Understanding how your sector is evolving can help you make sense of your own results and think about what comes next.
